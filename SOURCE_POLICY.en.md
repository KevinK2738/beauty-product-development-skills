# Source and Rights Policy

[简体中文](SOURCE_POLICY.md)

## Principle

The repository stores only the minimum independently written fact summary needed for a task. Public accessibility does not grant permission to republish an entire work. Unless an explicit license permits it, do not store third-party images, logos, full webpages, reports, long quotations, or raw datasets.

## Evidence record

Every public example records the source title, publisher, direct URL, access date, market, source type, the specific fact supported, a `fact`, `inference`, or `simulation` status, and any freshness or applicability limit.

Facts must be paraphrased independently. Inferences must be labelled and must not be presented as internal brand facts. Simulated assignments and recommendations must not impersonate real brand documents.

## Source priority

1. Regulation, standard, or official database.
2. Official brand, supplier, or retailer site.
3. Professional publication with identifiable author, date, and method.
4. Reviews and social content, used as problem-discovery leads rather than sole support for a firm conclusion.

Price, MOQ, lead time, inventory, certification, testing, and regulatory status can change. Mark them `requires-confirmation` unless a current source directly supports them.

## Trademarks and brands

Brand and product names identify public sources for educational purposes only. No affiliation, sponsorship, or endorsement is implied. Report rights concerns through a GitHub issue.

This policy is not legal advice. Contributors remain responsible for confirming the rights required to summarize, cite, and redistribute material. For China-market material, consult the [Copyright Law of the People's Republic of China published by the National Copyright Administration](https://www.ncac.gov.cn/xxfb/flfg/flfg_532/202103/t20210309_50530.html).
