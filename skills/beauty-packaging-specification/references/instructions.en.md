# Beauty Packaging Technical Specification: Professional Instructions

## Objective

Organize a selected or inquiry-stage packaging system into a versioned technical baseline with sources, states, and responsibility boundaries so that brand, supplier, engineering, quality, and procurement discuss the same object. Record current evidence; never fill missing engineering information.

## Declare one document state

- `inquiry`: confirm capability and commercial feasibility; many fields may remain open.
- `sample`: define sample type, purpose, and evaluation; not mass-production status.
- `confirmed`: include only specifications with a confirmation source; still not production release.
- `change`: record before and after, reason, impact, revalidation, and approval owner.

One document has one primary state. Record version, date, project, market, supplier or model, and input cut-off date.

## Components and sources

Build a complete BOM: formula-contact container, pump, valve, dropper or sprayer, cap, plug, wiper or applicator, gasket and seal, label or decoration, carton, insert, leaflet, and accessory. A catalog image showing assembled parts does not make them a confirmed complete assembly.

For every dimension, material, color, process, function, and commercial field, record value, unit, source document/page or reply, source date, evidence state, and owner. Image estimates and contextual inferences cannot become confirmed specifications.

## Samples versus production

Distinguish appearance sample, stock sample, handmade sample, functional sample, mold sample, pilot sample, and mass-production sample. A passing handmade sample does not prove automated production, batch color, decoration adhesion, or assembly stability. List pre-production validation separately.

## Tests and acceptance criteria

Record only applicable standard, method, condition, sample stage, acceptance criterion, report identifier, and owner. Mark a missing element to define or provide. A recommended test, supplier claim, or historical report is not a current-project pass.

## Change control

For changes to supplier, factory, mold, resin grade, contact layer, seal, pump or valve, decoration, color, capacity, or critical dimension, create a change record and identify affected drawings, samples, tests, quotations, and timing. Do not update the final baseline without accountable confirmation.

## Completion check

- The BOM does not omit labels, gaskets, inserts, or accessories.
- Critical fields have a source and state.
- Document state, sample type, and production state are not confused.
- The specification does not replace drawings, quality agreements, contracts, regulatory documents, or release signatures.
