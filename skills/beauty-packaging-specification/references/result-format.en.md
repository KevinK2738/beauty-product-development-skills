# Beauty Packaging Technical Specification Format

## Document control

Project, document state, version, date, market, supplier/model, requirements version, input cut-off, preparer, and confirmation owners.

## 1. Packaging system BOM

| BOM ID | Level | Component | Supplier part number | Function | Formula contact | Quantity | State |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---:|---|

## 2. Dimensions, capacity, and engineering assets

| BOM ID | Field | Value | Unit | Tolerance | Source | Drawing/version | State | Owner |
|---|---|---:|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Leave unconfirmed values blank and state the required source; do not fill by estimate.

## 3. Material, color, and process

| BOM ID | Material/grade | Contact layer | Color standard | Finish/decoration | Process location | Source | State |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 4. Function, assembly, and use

| Function | Target | Method/condition | Acceptance criterion | Sample stage | Current result | Evidence/report | State |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 5. Samples and delivery

| Sample type | Purpose | Quantity | Configuration/version | Delivery date | Evaluation owner | Does not represent |
|---|---|---:|---|---|---|---|

## 6. Commercial fields

MOQ, price tiers, tax and freight basis, tooling and plate fees, payment, quote validity, capacity, and lead time, each with date and applicable condition.

## 7. Open items and deviations

| ID | Open item/deviation | Impact | Temporary treatment | Required evidence | Owner | Due date |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 8. Change record

| Change ID | Date | Before | After | Reason | Affected asset/test | Approval state |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 9. Attachment index and confirmation

List filename, version, and state for drawings, color standards, sample photos, reports, quotations, and replies. State that the specification is not mass-production release.
