# Beauty Packaging Specification

Use this skill to create a controlled information baseline for inquiry, sampling, confirmation, or change.

## Language routing

- For Chinese tasks, read [references/instructions.zh-CN.md](references/instructions.zh-CN.md) and [references/result-format.zh-CN.md](references/result-format.zh-CN.md).
- For English tasks, read [references/instructions.en.md](references/instructions.en.md) and [references/result-format.en.md](references/result-format.en.md).
- Use the matching blank workbook in [assets/](assets/) when an editable spreadsheet is requested.

## Operating boundary

- Declare one document state: inquiry, sample, confirmed, or change.
- Record every dimension and material with its source and confirmation status.
- Keep hand samples, functional samples, pilot production, and mass production distinct.
- The specification does not replace final engineering drawings, quality agreements, contracts, regulatory documents, or signed release.
