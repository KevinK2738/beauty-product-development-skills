# Beauty Product Brief

Use this skill to define the product to be developed and the gates by which it will be validated.

## Language routing

- For Chinese tasks, read [references/instructions.zh-CN.md](references/instructions.zh-CN.md) and [references/result-format.zh-CN.md](references/result-format.zh-CN.md).
- For English tasks, read [references/instructions.en.md](references/instructions.en.md) and [references/result-format.en.md](references/result-format.en.md).
- Translate only after applying the same evidence and responsibility boundaries.

## Operating boundary

- Separate target experience and claims from achieved or substantiated facts.
- Preserve unresolved commercial, formulation, packaging, and regulatory decisions.
- Do not invent percentages, thresholds, costs, dates, MOQ, suppliers, or test results to make the brief look complete.
- Do not expand the assignment into launch management unless the user explicitly asks for that separate work.
