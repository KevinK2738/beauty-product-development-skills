# Beauty Product Development Brief Format

## Document control

Project, version, date, market, owner, and input cut-off date.

## 1. Decision summary

User problem, product proposition, critical trade-offs, and current decision state.

## 2. User and occasion

| User/occasion | Current problem | Desired change | Evidence state | Validation method |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## 3. Product definition

| Dimension | Target | Priority | Current basis | Validation gate | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Cover format, size, sensory target, use method, core value, and explicit non-goals.

## 4. Formula and claim direction

| Item | Target or intended expression | Current evidence | Required evidence/test | State | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 5. Packaging and use-experience tasks

Record problems the packaging system must solve, known constraints, requirements to derive, and matters that cannot yet be confirmed.

## 6. Commercial and launch boundary

Market, channel, price tier, target cost, first-order quantity, timing, and critical conflicts.

## 7. Risks, conflicts, and decisions

| Item | Impact | Current state | Next decision | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## 8. Validation plan

State minimum validation gates for formula, sensory experience, packaging, claims, and commercial assumptions.
