# Packaging Design Directions: Professional Instructions

## Objective

Create directions that product and design teams can choose and develop, grounded in product strategy, target user, brand constraints, and verifiable packaging structures. Directions must represent different strategic choices, not color or graphic swaps.

## Entry conditions

Require a clear product assignment, brand or expression boundary, and at least one real structure or a defined structural search range. Check source, components, capacity, visible surfaces, and known limits. With only inspiration images or no structure data, downgrade to concept exploration and do not imply sampling or production readiness.

## Build directions

- Identify a real decision tension, such as clinical efficacy versus sensory pleasure, home display versus portability, or conservative structural risk versus distinctive interaction.
- For each direction, define audience and occasion, core promise, structural use, CMF, graphic and information hierarchy, opening and use experience, retained brand assets, risks, and validation work.
- Let the number of directions follow the real tension. Output one when only one is valid and usually no more than three.
- A distinct direction must differ across at least two of strategy, structural use, interaction, or information architecture. Color, pattern, or naming alone is not enough.

## Visual execution

Deliver or claim visual files only when suitable image or 3D tools are available, asset use is authorized, and the generated result has been inspected. Otherwise provide a structured creative specification for a designer or generation tool and mark it `recommendation`.

Do not imitate a recognizable brand style or generate unauthorized trademarks, people, or protected assets. Translate references into general design principles and cite the source.

## Feasibility boundary

Visual plausibility does not confirm process, compatibility, cost, lead time, or mass-production feasibility. List supplier, engineering, testing, and regulatory confirmation items for each direction.

## Completion check

- Directions differ strategically.
- Immutable geometry and assembly relationships of the real structure are preserved.
- Visual-delivery claims match actual tool results.
- The recommendation includes rationale, cost of choice, and next review question.
