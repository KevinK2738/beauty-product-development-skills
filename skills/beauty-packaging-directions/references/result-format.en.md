# Packaging Design Directions Format

## 1. Decision context

Product assignment, user or occasion, brand constraints, structure source, current evidence, and the tension to decide.

## 2. Direction overview

| Direction | Strategic choice | Occasion | Structural strategy | Material difference | Main cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 3. Direction detail

For each direction include:

1. Core proposition and target user.
2. Product and packaging role.
3. Use of the real structure.
4. CMF and tactile intent.
5. Graphic, information, and claim hierarchy.
6. Opening, dispensing, and use experience.
7. Visual-asset status.
8. Risks, unknowns, and validation tasks.

## 4. Direction comparison

| Decision dimension | Direction A | Direction B | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|

## 5. Recommendation and change list

Recommended direction, rationale, value given up, and items to confirm or modify next.

## 6. Matters not confirmed

Process, color consistency, material, compatibility, cost, lead time, regulatory, and production status.
