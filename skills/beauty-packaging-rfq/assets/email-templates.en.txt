Beauty Packaging Supplier Email Templates (English)
Author: K-Beauty

Use principles
- Select only the mode needed for the current task.
- Before sending, the project owner must confirm the recipient, controlled specification version, attachments, quantities, dates, and authority.
- Delete inapplicable fields. A blank response is not supplier acceptance.

2. RFQ
Subject: [RFQ][Project code][Specification version][Quantity tiers][Validity requirement]

Hello,
Please quote item by item against the attached controlled specification. This RFQ is not an order or acceptance of terms.

State: quotation reference and date, specification version, quantity tiers, unit price and currency, tax basis, Incoterms rule + named place + version, freight and packaging, tooling/plates, sample fees, MOQ, payment terms, sample and production lead times, quotation validity, deviations, and exclusions.

List any alternative material, structure, or process as a deviation; do not assume equivalence.
