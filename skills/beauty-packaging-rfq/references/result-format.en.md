# Packaging Inquiry and Response Alignment Pack Format

## A. Control information

Project, inquiry ID, mode, date, specification version, supplier, response deadline, currency, tax and freight basis, and attachments.

## B. RFI only

| Question ID | Required capability/information | Component/model | Required evidence | Date requirement | Supplier response | State |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## C. RFQ only

### Specification and quantity basis

| BOM ID | Component/specification | Version | Quantity scenario | Permitted deviation | Attachment |
|---|---|---|---:|---|---|

### Quotation response

| BOM/set | Quantity tier | Unit price | Currency | Tax | Freight/Incoterm | MOQ | Tooling/plate fee | Validity | Lead time |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|---|---:|---:|---|---|

## D. Sampling request only

| Sample type | Purpose | Configuration/version | Quantity | Medium | Color/process | Evaluation | Delivery | Does not represent |
|---|---|---|---:|---|---|---|---|---|

## E. Response alignment only

| Requested field | Requested value/version | Original response and location | Normalized value | State | Deviation/impact | Follow-up |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## F. Open items and ownership

| ID | Open item | Comparison/schedule impact | Required reply or evidence | Owner | Due date |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## G. Pre-send check

Confirm mode, specification version, quantity, currency and tax/freight basis, attachments, recipient, and authorization. State: "Draft only; not sent automatically."
