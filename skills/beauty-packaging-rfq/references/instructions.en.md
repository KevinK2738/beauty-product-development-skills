# Packaging RFI, RFQ, Sampling, and Response Alignment: Professional Instructions

## Objective

Select one mode for the current supplier task and prepare comparable, fillable, and traceable communication material. A draft prepares content only; it does not authorize sending or acceptance of terms.

## Mode selection

### RFI: information and capability request

Use when it is not yet known whether a supplier has the required structure, material, process, production line, test evidence, or regional supply capability. Ask for verifiable evidence, applicable model, and date rather than an irrelevant full company profile.

### RFQ: request for quotation

Use when specification and quantity bases support comparable quotation. State project and specification version, component BOM, annual or order scenario, price tiers, currency, tax, freight, Incoterms where relevant, tooling or plate fee, MOQ, payment, quote validity, samples, and lead time.

### Sampling request

Define sample type, purpose, configuration, quantity, color and decoration, formula or substitute medium, sample stage, evaluation items, delivery form, and what the sample does not represent. A stock sample is not a functional sample; a handmade sample does not represent production stability.

### Response alignment

Map each supplier response to the requested version. Preserve original wording or attachment location, normalized value, basis, state, and deviation. No explicit reply means not answered; "can do" does not imply complete compliance.

## Shared control fields

Project, inquiry ID, mode, date, specification version, supplier, contact only if provided by the user, deadline, currency, tax and freight basis, confidentiality or use limit, and attachment index.

## Comparability and deviations

- Price must match the same components, specification version, quantity tier, currency, tax and freight basis, and tooling amortization.
- Record valid date and conditions for MOQ, price, lead time, and capacity.
- Permit supplier alternatives, but list deviation, impact, and re-review separately.
- Do not invent contractual responsibility for quality issues, rework, scrap, resampling, or delay; raise it for procurement or legal confirmation.

## Permissions and boundary

Do not search for or invent contacts, send messages, promise orders, forecasts, target prices, payment, or timing, or perform supplier approval, contract negotiation, test approval, or production release.

## Completion check

- Only the needed mode is executed.
- Request and response bind to one specification version.
- Price, sample, and delivery bases are comparable.
- Missing, deviating, stale, and supplier-asserted fields retain their real state.
