# Competitor and Product-Packaging Opportunity Brief Format

## 1. Decision question and basis

Market, user or occasion, category, channel, price basis, cut-off date, and exclusions.

## 2. Executive conclusion

Separate supported conclusions, inferences, and questions that cannot currently be answered.

## 3. Competitor fact comparison

| Product and version | Market/channel | Size | Public claims | Experience signals | Packaging system | Source and date |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 4. Normalized price and size

| Product | Regular/promo price | Size | Price per unit | Currency, tax, freight basis | Date | Comparability limit |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---|---|

## 5. User-problem evidence

| Problem signal | Sample and channel | Repetition/materiality | Counterexample or bias | State |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## 6. Candidate opportunities and barriers

| Opportunity hypothesis | Supporting evidence | Counter-evidence | Brand/capability fit | Barrier | Validation step |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 7. Decision recommendation

Recommend enter, pause, or continue research and state evidence strength and stopping conditions.
