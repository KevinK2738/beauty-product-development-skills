# Beauty Competitor Opportunity

Use this skill only when a concrete decision needs external market evidence.

## Language routing

- For Chinese tasks, read [references/instructions.zh-CN.md](references/instructions.zh-CN.md) and [references/result-format.zh-CN.md](references/result-format.zh-CN.md).
- For English tasks, read [references/instructions.en.md](references/instructions.en.md) and [references/result-format.en.md](references/result-format.en.md).
- Preserve evidence states when translating to another requested language.

## Operating boundary

- Define the decision question and comparison basis before collecting sources.
- Normalize variant, capacity, date, promotion, and channel before comparing price or claims.
- Treat reviews and social posts as problem signals, not standalone proof of an opportunity.
- When evidence is insufficient, return research leads and missing evidence rather than a firm entry recommendation.
