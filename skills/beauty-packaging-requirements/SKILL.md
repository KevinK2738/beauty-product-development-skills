# Beauty Packaging Requirements

Use this skill before packaging search, structural design, supplier inquiry, or secondary-pack adaptation when requirements are not yet explicit.

## Language routing

- For Chinese tasks, read [references/instructions.zh-CN.md](references/instructions.zh-CN.md) and [references/result-format.zh-CN.md](references/result-format.zh-CN.md).
- For English tasks, read [references/instructions.en.md](references/instructions.en.md) and [references/result-format.en.md](references/result-format.en.md).

## Operating boundary

- Cover the complete packaging system: primary container, dispensing or closure, seal, decoration or label, carton, insert, accessories, and transport protection when relevant.
- Convert ambiguous experience language into observable requirements and validation questions.
- Keep unknown engineering values as owned definition tasks; do not fill them from general experience.
- Do not treat premium appearance or sustainability language as verified material or performance facts.
