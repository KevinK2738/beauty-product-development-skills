# Beauty Packaging Requirements: Professional Instructions

## Objective

Translate product and commercial inputs into functional, experiential, visual, manufacturing, logistics, and validation requirements for the complete packaging system. The result supports candidate search, structural design, and supplier communication; it is not a confirmed engineering specification.

## Establish input maturity

Record formula format, rheology or viscosity signals, sensitivities, contact restrictions, capacity, dose pattern, use environment, channel, transport, cost, and timing. Mark unknowns `requires-confirmation` and state the affected component or test.

## Packaging system scope

Cover as relevant:

- primary container, liner, or pouch in direct formula contact;
- pump, sprayer, dropper, valve, cap, plug, wiper, roller, or applicator;
- gasket, seal, lock, tamper evidence, and accidental-actuation control;
- label, direct print, decoration, and variable information;
- carton, insert, leaflet, accessory, and set packaging;
- ecommerce, retail, travel, and transport protection.

Do not omit the rest of the system because the user mentioned only a bottle.

## Derive requirements

- Record each requirement's triggering input, statement, priority, validation method, and owner.
- Convert premium, light, clean, leakproof, or easy-to-press language into observable operation or review criteria.
- Separate Must, Should, and Could. Hard gates must originate in real product, regulatory, channel, or engineering constraints.
- Material identity, contact safety, recyclability, PCR content, and environmental claims require evidence and cannot be inferred visually.
- Light, oxygen, moisture, volatility, corrosion, staining, and odor risks create protection requirements and test plans; they do not prove compatibility.

## Engineering and validation boundary

Without an applicable standard, drawing, or owner confirmation, do not fill tolerances, torque, dose, drop height, temperature, duration, or acceptance thresholds. State parameters to define, recommended test type, and sample stage instead.

Primary-pack dimensions alone do not support production-ready carton dimensions. Require final drawings, assembly allowance, material thickness, insert, and transport conditions.

## Completion check

- Every relevant component is covered.
- Every hard requirement has an input basis.
- Experience language is converted into observable requirements.
- The validation plan distinguishes to define, to execute, and passed, with no invented results.
