# Beauty Packaging Requirements Format

## Document information

Project, version, date, product format and size, market, and input maturity.

## 1. Packaging strategy summary

Core use occasion, problems to solve, critical trade-offs, and matters not yet confirmed.

## 2. Formula and contact system

| Input/risk | Affected component | Packaging requirement | State | Validation plan | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 3. Use experience and function

| User action | Target experience | Observable requirement | Priority | Validation method |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## 4. Packaging system components

| Level | Component | Function | Known constraint | Parameter to define |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## 5. Brand, CMF, and information

State requirements and evidence status for color, material appearance, finish, copy hierarchy, marks, and variable information.

## 6. Channel, logistics, and manufacturing

Retail, ecommerce, sets, transport, filling and assembly, production-line, and quality-control requirements.

## 7. Commercial and project boundary

Cost, first-order quantity, timing, region, existing supplier constraints, and unconfirmed commercial information.

## 8. Requirement and validation matrix

| ID | Requirement | Must/Should/Could | Source | Sample stage | Validation owner | Current state |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 9. Blockers and next step

State why candidate selection, design, or production-dimension confirmation cannot yet proceed.
