# Packaging Candidate Review Format

## 1. Review task and data scope

Project, requirement version, candidate sources, material cut-off date, and unavailable material.

## 2. Conclusion

Candidates that pass, conditionally pass, cannot be verified, or fail, and whether a recommendation is supportable.

## 3. Candidate identity and components

| Candidate | Supplier/source | Model or link | Component scope | Material date | Identity completeness |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 4. Hard-gate check

| Requirement | Candidate A | Candidate B | Candidate C | Evidence source | Conclusion |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Use pass, non-conforming, or not verifiable; do not replace hard gates with a vague total score.

## 5. Trade-off comparison

| Dimension | Candidate | Advantage | Cost/limit | State | Confirmation item |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 6. Commercials and freshness

| Candidate | MOQ | Price basis | Sample | Lead time/inventory | Source date | Current usability |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 7. Recommendation and next step

State recommendation, conditions, and stop point. If no candidates exist, provide search conditions and RFI fields instead.
