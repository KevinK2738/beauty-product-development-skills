# Packaging Candidate Review: Professional Instructions

## Objective

Review user-identified packaging candidates for hard constraints, evidence completeness, use experience, design, manufacturing, and commercial trade-offs. State which candidates may continue, why, and what is missing. Never create a supplier or model that is not in the input.

## Data admission

Each candidate needs an identifiable name, model, or page; supplier or source; component scope; and material date. Images and renderings may support visual observation but do not prove material, capacity, dimensions, function, manufacturability, or commercial terms.

Mark fields as documented by a catalog, drawing, or supplier source; confirmed by the user; inferred from image or context; or missing/stale. An inference cannot pass a hard gate.

## Review order

1. Extract Must gates and Should/Could trade-offs from the requirements.
2. Check identity, component completeness, critical-dimension source, formula-contact material, capacity or dose information, sealing and locking, protection, and decoration capability.
3. A missing hard-gate field is not a pass; mark it not verifiable. Reject an explicit non-conformance.
4. For candidates that pass hard gates, compare use experience, brand expression, process freedom, filling and assembly, logistics, and commercial risk.
5. If only one candidate qualifies, say so; never lower the gate to produce a Top 3.

## Commercial freshness

Record source date and applicable conditions for MOQ, price, sampling fee, inventory, capacity, lead time, and certification. Historical values create reconfirmation questions only. A supplier statement is not procurement approval, a test pass, or a contract commitment.

## Degraded result when no candidates exist

Do not output invented candidates. Provide search hard conditions, permitted trade-offs, required catalog or drawing fields, and supplier RFI fields. Do not contact suppliers.

## Completion check

- Every candidate traces to a real input.
- Hard gates, unknowns, and soft trade-offs are separate.
- Recommendations include fit, cost of the choice, risk, and confirmation step.
- Stale data is not presented as current fact.
